
# Approved Tools Policy

VisibleHand utilizes a suite of approved software tools for internal use by workforce members. These software tools are either self-hosted, with security managed by VisibleHand, or they are hosted by a Subcontractor with appropriate business associate agreements in place to preserve data integrity. Use of other tools requires approval from VisibleHand leadership.

## List of Approved Tools

* **GitHub**. GitHub is used for source and version control of application code, firmware, and infrastructure-as-code used by VisibleHand, as well as for automated build and deployment workflows (GitHub Actions). No ePHI is stored in GitHub.

* **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. AWS hosts all VisibleHand production infrastructure, including compute, storage, databases, and IoT services. VisibleHand maintains a business associate agreement with AWS. AWS S3 is used for storage of files and, where appropriate, sharing of files with Partners and Customers.

* **Google Workspace**. Google Gmail is used for email. Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets are used for storage of internal files and sharing of files with Partners and Customers.

* **Slack**. Slack is used for internal team communication. Workforce members may not place ePHI in Slack messages or channels.

* **Linear**. Linear is used for issue tracking and project management. Workforce members may not place ePHI in Linear issues.

* **Intercom**. Intercom is used for Customer support communication. Support conversations are limited to the minimum necessary information.
